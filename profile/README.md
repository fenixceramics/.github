🐦‍🔥 _Like a phoenix reborn, we transform bottom ash into high-value ceramic materials_ 🐦‍🔥

We are Fenix Ceramics, a circular manufacturing and technology start-up funded in 2024, with a mission to produce composite ceramics from common incinerator byproducts, combining technological innovation with environmental sustainability. By converting a typical industrial residue into safe, durable, and design-driven products, we aim to contribute to a circular and resource-efficient materials economy.

Our vision is to redefine waste as a material resource, accelerating the adoption of circular materials that create economic value by reducing environmental impact. Through a multidisciplinary approach, bridging material science and process engineering, we are driven to reshape how composite ceramics are produced and used at scale.
